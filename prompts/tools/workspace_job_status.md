Check on a background job: its state (running, exited, cancelled, timed_out, lost), exit code once it has ended, elapsed time and the tail of its output. It waits up to waitSeconds (default {{default_wait}}, at most {{max_wait}}) and answers as soon as the job ends, so call it in a loop rather than sleeping: {{default_wait}} s for the first few checks, then longer (120, then {{max_wait}}) for a job that runs on. Between checks, write one short progress line for the thread (elapsed time and what the output shows, e.g. "4m in, 12 of 40 benchmarks done"), at most one a minute, and nothing when there is no news. Every few checks, read the thread (slack_read_thread) for new messages: if someone asks to stop the job, call workspace_job_cancel with that message's ts. When it ends, report the result: exit code, how long it took, and what the output says (the numbers, or the failures). A lost job stopped with its sandbox and did not finish: say so.
